03. METHODOLOGY // SLOW ATELIER

From raw cord to sculpted form.

Mass-market bags peel after one season. Every Adoness piece is tensioned on wooden jigs, woven by hand, and lined for longevity—or built around a beloved bag you send in for a complete woven rebirth.
